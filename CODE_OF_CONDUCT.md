# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Open-IPv8-Lab a respectful, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Focus feedback on code, ideas, and outcomes, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for contributors of all experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, intimidation, or repeated hostile behavior.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:

- contact@alekseialeinikov.com

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement actions

Depending on severity and frequency, maintainers may apply one or more of the following:

- Warning
- Temporary suspension from project spaces
- Permanent ban from project interaction

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open-source community standards and adapted for Open-IPv8-Lab.
